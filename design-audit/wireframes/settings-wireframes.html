Astra Settings, three directions per platform

Grayscale wireframes for design-audit/05-settings-directions.md. Same settings, same words, three different structures. Dashed orange boxes are annotations, not UI. On Apple TV the black filled row is the remote's focus; a tick is the saved choice. Nothing here is built.

iPhone A. Ultra-native, minimal

Apple's own Settings pattern: one grouped list, every row shows its current value, sub-pages are native forms with footers. Least "Astra", most predictable.
